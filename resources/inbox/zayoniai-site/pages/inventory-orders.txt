## Example: a grocery store repeatedly running out of essentials 
The challenge: Popular products run out before the purchasing manager notices. 
Set reorder thresholds for critical products. 
Read sales and quantity changes from a connected stock system. 
Trigger an alert and proposed reorder amount when stock drops. 
A manager checks the count and approves the purchase. 
Potential outcome: Potential benefit: earlier warnings and clearer order statuses, provided physical counts and records are reliable.
