FAQ

What to know before you start

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

Usually not. We connect Zayoni to your existing WhatsApp Business number, or set up a new one if you prefer.

Does it really speak Iraqi Arabic?

Yes. We test it on real questions from your customers before launch and tune its tone until it sounds like your own staff.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It never guesses. It tells the customer a team member will follow up and hands the conversation over with a summary.

How long until it's live?

Usually within 7 days of the first call, often faster for small businesses.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can stop any time. Choose yearly and get 2 months free.

How do I pay?

In USD or Iraqi dinar, via local payment methods or bank transfer. We'll agree on it during the call.

How is Zayoni different from other chatbots?

We don't hand you a tool and leave. We set everything up, train your team, and keep improving it with you every month.
