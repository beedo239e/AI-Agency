# Vision

> Status: draft — v0 derived from the current site (zayoniai.com). See `resources/notes/zayoniai-site.md`.

## One-liner
_Working draft:_ Zayoni helps small and mid-size businesses use AI assistants to reply to customers,
book appointments, follow up sales, and cut routine work — with humans approving what matters.

Current tagline: «خلي الذكاء الاصطناعي يشتغل وياك. مو بدالك.» (AI works *with* you, not instead of you.)

## Problem
SMB owners lose customers and time to: slow/missed WhatsApp replies and calls, scattered leads,
messy appointments, manual reports, and staff who don't know how to use AI tools.

## Target customer
- Current site: **Iraqi** SMBs — clinics, real estate, restaurants/cafés, retail, factories/distribution, services/education.
- _Open: Iraq only, or also Egypt / Gulf? (see open questions)_

## Solution / what the agency offers
AI assistants and automations set up and run for the client (done-for-you), across 9 areas:
WhatsApp & customer service, voice assistant, email & appointments, sales/CRM, marketing content,
accounting & reporting, inventory & orders, internal workflows, team training.
