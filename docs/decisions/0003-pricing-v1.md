# 0003 — Pricing v1 (draft, shown on the website)

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Status:** proposed — founders to confirm before launch

## Decision
Setup fee (one-time) + monthly subscription, USD (payable in IQD). Yearly = 10 months paid.

| Plan | Monthly | Setup | Conversations | Key features |
|------|---------|-------|---------------|--------------|
| Starter | $49 | $99 | 1,000 | WhatsApp, FAQ answers, handoff, monthly report |
| Growth ⭐ | $99 | $149 | 3,000 | + Instagram/Messenger, bookings & reminders, dashboard, weekly report, 3 users |
| Business | $199 | $299 | 10,000 | + multi-branch, integrations, monthly optimization call |
| Custom | quote | — | — | custom automation, 1:1 training |

Add-ons: Website $19/mo (+setup), Social content $79/mo, Google reviews $29/mo, Owner report $39/mo, Voice from $79/mo (+usage), Training $49/hour.

## Reasoning
- Entry price low enough for Iraqi SMBs; setup fee pays for onboarding time and filters non-serious leads.
- Running cost per client is a few dollars/month (see `docs/04-tools-and-subscriptions.md`) → gross margin ~85–90% on Starter/Growth.
- Growth is the anchor plan (marked "most popular"); Business exists to make Growth look reasonable and to capture multi-branch clients.
